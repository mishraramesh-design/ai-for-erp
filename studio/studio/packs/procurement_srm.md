# Procurement and Supplier Relationship Management: domain notes

Typical processes: supplier registration and onboarding (documents, eligibility, due diligence), empanelment and approval, product registration, price discovery and revision, purchase order and delivery, performance and scorecard, grievance and change requests, bank guarantee and security deposit tracking, supplier portal.

Questions a good consultant asks:
- Registration: which documents are mandatory per supplier category, who verifies them, what is the validity and renewal cycle, what happens on expiry (block, warn).
- Approval: how many levels, by value or category, who can override, SLA per level.
- Pricing: who can revise price, what evidence is needed, is there a maximum change percentage, are branch-level prices different, effective dates.
- Bank guarantee: amount rule, validity, release or encashment trigger, alert days before expiry, link to the Purchase Order or the supplier.
- Portal: what can a supplier see and edit, how is data isolated per supplier, what notifications go out.
- Performance: criteria and weights, review period, consequence of a low score.
- Audit: what must be logged, retention, who can see the log.
